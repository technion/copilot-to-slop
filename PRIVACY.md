# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 6/1/2026

## Overview

This Chrome extension is designed with your privacy in mind. It does **not** collect, store, process, or transmit any personal data or usage information from your device.

## Data Collection

This extension **does not collect any data** of any kind, including but not limited to:

- Personal information
- Browsing history
- Website content
- Cookies
- IP addresses
- Device or system information
- Usage or analytics data

## Data Transmission

The extension **does not transmit any data** from your machine to external servers or third parties. All functionality is performed locally within your browser.

## Third Parties

This extension:

- Does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services
- Does not share data with any third parties
- Does not embed external scripts that collect or transmit data

## Permissions

Any permissions requested by the extension are used **solely to provide its core functionality** and are not used to collect or transmit user data.

## Data Storage

The extension:

- Does not store personal data locally or remotely
- Does not use cookies, local storage, or other persistent storage mechanisms for tracking purposes

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated in the future, the changes will be reflected in this document with an updated revision date. Because no data is collected, any updates will not affect your privacy.
